## Brief: the Warcraft III TD veteran, a base for an AI playtester persona

Researched online on 1 November 2026, the base of every persona in `../personas/` (the owner's brief: long years of Warcraft III custom-map tower defense are the crucial base).

**Sourcing note.** I could not read Reddit (fetching is blocked) or Hive Workshop (a bot check I did not try to get past). The community voice therefore comes from Steam reviews and threads, the EleTD forums, ENT Gaming's Legion TD league forum, Legion TD's own changelog, the map archives (Epic War, W3Reforged, wc3maps) and some articles. Maul Tactics articles are written by a competing studio, and they say so themselves. "(snippet)" means I saw the text in search results but the page would not load. **[Inference]** marks my own reading.

### 1. The canon a veteran played

- **Element TD** (Karawasa, from 2006; [team page](https://www.eletd.com/team)).
  - **Elements:** there are six, in a cycle, and each armour takes more damage from the element before it: "Fire > Nature > Earth > Light > Dark > Water > Fire" ([EleTD forum](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/3392-why-element-td-is-awesome/)). The map was built by renaming WC3's own armour and attack types, so a matching type did "100%, 50% or 200%" ([EleTD forum](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/271-element-td-how-things-work/)). You pick elements during the match and combine them into dual and triple towers. You have 50 lives ([GIGA](https://www.giga.de/tipp/die-beliebtesten-tower-defense-maps-im-ueberblick-warcraft-3-reforged/)).
  - **Switches** were the culture: mazing or non-mazing, Hero mode, Race, Extreme, random elements, chaos (random wave order), "-harder" ([3392](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/3392-why-element-td-is-awesome/)). League rules read "Very Hard difficulty, pick, non-chaos, non-(extra) short, non-extreme" ([945](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/945-basics-of-element-td/)).
  - **Creep classes** each carry a rule: Fast (+25% speed), Healing, Mechanical (invulnerable for 3 s every 12 s), Undead (revives once at 33%) ([945](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/945-basics-of-element-td/)).
  - **Loved:** "The multiple elements/combo thing remains probably the coolest idea I've seen in TD" ([255](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/255-how-did-you-discover-element-td/page/2/)).
  - **Hated:** "hard to learn"; "balanced around non-mazing"; an entrance-camping build that "invoked too much rage" ([3392](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/3392-why-element-td-is-awesome/)).
- **Legion TD** (Lisk, 2009). In its day only DotA was more popular ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/legion-td-2/release)).
  - **Core:** fixed lanes and no mazing ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/games-like-legion-td-2)). Gold buys fighters, wisps gather lumber, and lumber pays for sends and King upgrades ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_TD)).
  - **Leaks:** creeps that leak to the King area give no bounty, and fighters warp to the King to catch them.
  - **The changelog** reads like a list of what veterans asked for ([changelog](http://legiontd.com/map/oldchangelog.php)):
    - Modes: -ap, -ar, -ni (1.30); -sm short and -mm Mastermind, "Restricted vision, limited scoreboard information" (1.40); -rd draft and -fr, "No two players will have the same race" (1.50).
    - Information: a "-next" command that "displays attack/armor type of the next wave", and greyed-out leavers (1.28); "attack & armor types in all summons' tooltips" (1.26).
    - Rules: "Disabled giving gold & lumber" (1.25); "If a player leaves, his spawn is now turned off" (1.22).
    - The end scoreboard was "modeled after that in DotA", with Leaks and Leaks Caught.
- **Wintermaul, Wintermaul One, Wintermaul Wars.**
  - **Wintermaul** is co-op maze survival. Anti-block rules apply, and some versions had a bypass "gold gate" you had to wall first. "Every creep that leaks costs you a life", and a teammate's leak hit the whole team.
  - **Wintermaul Wars** added sends that "raised your own income for the rest of the match". Dozens of near-identical versions were in circulation ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/wintermaul-wars-history)).
  - **Wintermaul One** has 49 races, 40 waves, and modes such as all random, all the same constructor and infinite waves ([wintermaul.one](https://wintermaul.one/)). GIGA's description: "Die ersten Male werdet ihr scheitern" ("the first few times you will fail"; [GIGA](https://www.giga.de/tipp/die-beliebtesten-tower-defense-maps-im-ueberblick-warcraft-3-reforged/)).
- **Line Tower Wars / Teamline TW.** Each player has a lane, and sends steal lives from whoever you send to. Income comes about every 15 s, and strong sends are time-gated. Teams split into "sender" and "builder" roles, and one exploit "pretty much broke the game" ([blog](https://postmasterinterest.blogspot.com/2013/06/warcraft-3-reminiscence-teamline-tower.html)). Matches run very long ([GIGA](https://www.giga.de/tipp/die-beliebtesten-tower-defense-maps-im-ueberblick-warcraft-3-reforged/)). The Reforged version added catch-up gold, sudden death at 25:30, a "-dls" detailed leaks/steals command and anti-block ([W3Reforged](https://maps.w3reforged.com/featured-maps/line-tower-wars-reforged)).
- **Green TD family.** The original has 60 waves ([GIGA](https://www.giga.de/tipp/die-beliebtesten-tower-defense-maps-im-ueberblick-warcraft-3-reforged/)). Remasters have air, immune, boss and hero waves and custom builders ([wc3maps](https://wc3maps.com/map/235040), snippet). In Green Circle TD you lose when the creeps alive reach a limit, not when lives run out ([Softpedia](https://games.softpedia.com/get/Shareware-Games/Warcraft-3-Map-Green-Circle-TD.shtml), snippet).
- **Burbenog TD** (Shvegait). Each player picks a governor "which determines the types of towers able to be built and the guardians able to be summoned", and "Teamwork is essential!". Its rating is 446 good to 69 bad ([Epic War](https://www.epicwar.com/maps/973/)), with 12 governors and 40+ levels ([W3Reforged](https://maps.w3reforged.com/featured-maps/burbenog-td)). It is Groundhold's closest ancestor.
- **Gem TD.** Each round you place five random gems and keep one; the others become maze stones. Recipes combine gems ([RAWG](https://rawg.io/games/gemtd)). Players blame the air bosses for its imbalance, not the maze (Steam thread, snippet).
- **YouTD** (geX). Random tower rolls from the elements you research, items and oils ("Item find/quality" is called the most important thing), and builders with their own traits ([youtd.best](https://youtd.best/howtoplay.html)).
- **Pokemon Defense.** You catch Pokemon as towers and trade-evolve them at level 100 with -ta ([Hive](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/resources/pd-3-9-e-eng.86751), snippet).
- **Send-economy cousins.**
  - Castle Fight: buildings spawn units ([SC2Mapster](https://sc2mapster.com/projects/castle-fight)), with a 3v3 ranked ladder on W3Champions (snippet).
  - Hero Line Wars: you buy units with kill gold and send them ([GIGA](https://www.giga.de/tipp/die-beliebtesten-tower-defense-maps-im-ueberblick-warcraft-3-reforged/)).
  - Footman Frenzy: a hero plus a constant spawn ([RankedBoost](https://rankedboost.com/warcraft-3/custom-maps/)).
  - Builder-versus-hunter maps: Vampirism (a killed human turns vampire; [Hive](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/vampirism-nightmare.130522), snippet) and Island Defense (a killed Builder becomes a Titan minion; [SC2Mapster remake](https://www.sc2mapster.com/projects/island-defense-dawn-new-age)).
- **Island TD:** I found no source. Treat it as unverified.
- **Lineage:** Desktop Tower Defense grew out of WC3's "Autumn Tower Defense" map ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desktop_Tower_Defense)).

### 2. Vocabulary and habits

- **Economy and pressure:**
  - Leaking: leak, "Leaks Caught" ([changelog](http://legiontd.com/map/oldchangelog.php)).
  - Value: "Recommended value is an approximation of how much fighter value that you need to clear the wave" ([LTD2 manual](https://beta.legiontd2.com/manual/)).
  - Sends: income sends versus power sends, which "give reduced income"; saving across waves ([wiki](https://legiontd2.wiki.gg/wiki/Mercenary)).
  - Sending for income every wave: "This is the unspoken meta of Classic" ([dev](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/3830828948526350151)).
  - Feeding, "outrolled", "yolo" leaks ([ENT](https://entgaming.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=52644)).
- **Placement:**
  - Mazing, juggling ("alternating between barricading an exit on one side and then the other side"; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_defense)).
  - Anti-block, chokepoint, kill-box, slows and splash "where creeps bunch up", sell penalties against juggling ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/mazing-guide)).
- **WC3 damage types:**
  - Attack types are Normal, Piercing, Magic, Siege, Hero and Chaos; armour types are Light, Medium, Heavy, Fortified, Unarmored and Hero.
  - Key multipliers: Piercing does 200% to Light and 35% to Fortified; Siege 150% to Fortified; Magic 200% to Heavy; Chaos 100% to all ([warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Warcraft_III_armor_and_attack_types)).
- **Mode strings:** -ap, -ar, -rd, -sm, -mm, -hg; -next, -value, -king ([changelog](http://legiontd.com/map/oldchangelog.php)); lobby codes such as "PRCCX3" and "2K+" ([legionladder](https://legionladder.com/blog/how-to-play-legion-td-warcraft-3-reforged)).
- **Interface:**
  - The command card is a 4x3 grid ([Blizzard forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/warcraft3/t/eu-grid-keybinds/11348)). Players talk of "pre-set QWER aligned hotkeys": "why would you not use the grid alignment??" ([Blizzard forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/warcraft3/t/custom-hotkeys/16020)).
  - The multiboard and quest-log entries ([changelog](http://legiontd.com/map/oldchangelog.php)).
  - Shift-click to queue several builds ([classic Battle.net guide mirror](https://classic.hiveworkshop.com/war3/basics/buildings.shtml), snippet).
  - **[Inference]** A veteran types a -zoom-style camera command first (a Hive tutorial builds one, [snippet](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/how-to-move-camera-to-see-the-sky.343970)).
- **Social:**
  - Follow "the team caller's concise send or King instructions"; "The match is won as a team, not by having the prettiest individual lane" ([legionladder](https://legionladder.com/blog/how-to-play-legion-td-warcraft-3-reforged)).
  - Coordination was typed: "We coordinated by typing" ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/wintermaul-wars-history)). "noob" was a common insult ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/3830828948526350151)).
  - **[Inference]** "gg" or "gl hf" opens and closes a match.

### 3. Expectations, and what makes them quit

**Expects:**
- **The next wave's armour, attack type and special shown before it arrives**, as Legion's -next and its tooltip types did.
- **A sense of whether their defence is enough** (value, or recommended value).
- **Mode switches and difficulty ladders**, plus random and draft picks.
- **Stats at the end** (leaks, leaks caught, gold from kills).
- **Standalone basics:** reconnecting, matchmaking and a playable AI. The LTD2 developer named exactly these as what the standalone adds ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/595140101859705618/)).
- **Hotkeys everywhere.** Legion added summon hotkeys and removed its Sell hotkey ([changelog](http://legiontd.com/map/oldchangelog.php)); **[Inference]** the Sell hotkey went to prevent misclicks.

**Quits over:**
- **Snowballing.** Games were "one-sided from wave 5", where in WC3 "most games made it at least to wave 18"; "When you leak you give the enemy money" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/3830828948526350151)). Another review: "If you are matched against a team with more experience or knowledge of build lists, you will lose" ([LTD2 negative reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/negativereviews/?p=1&browsefilter=toprated)).
- **Solved builds.** "mass workers, enable auto-send, and win by mindless eco" ([LTD2 negative reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/negativereviews/?p=1&browsefilter=toprated)). In Element TD 2, players want the developers to "nerf 6-ele, the strongest build", and one wrote "There are no strategic decisions to be made" ([ETD2 negative reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1018830/negativereviews/?p=1&browsefilter=toprated)).
- **RNG.** "you will have 5 diffrent outcomes" (LTD2 negative reviews).
- **Teammates.** Toxicity and smurfs (LTD2 negative reviews). Deliberate clogging and leaking: "Thats just no way to determine the game outcome" ([ENT](https://entgaming.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=52644)). "a slightly under-performing ally can doom your game" ([Save or Quit](https://saveorquit.com/2021/10/29/review-legion-td-2/)).
- **Leavers.** In the WC3 map a leaver's lane simply went dark. LTD2 added an auto-AFK cancel and leaver penalties, including one for a player who left while still choosing towers ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/1489992080520914881/)).
- **Clarity.** "the UI isn't clear about how things actually work" (ETD2 negative reviews); "getting stomped for probably like the first 20-30h" (LTD2 negative reviews).
- **Netcode.** In Wintermaul Wars, players leaked "and blamed lag" ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/wintermaul-wars-history)). In Element TD 2, "a lot of lag, and frequent disconnects" (ETD2 negative reviews). WC3's own lag window was a countdown to drop the lagger ([BNetDocs](https://bnetdocs.org/packet/519/w3gs-start-lag)). LTD2 players also complain about it being always online.
- **Exploitable pathing.** Players used angled walls that "exploited quirks" in the creeps' pathing ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=20974461)).
- **Overlong matches and empty lobbies.** "The population keeps declining" (LTD2 negative reviews).

### 4. Modern successors on Steam

- **Legion TD 2:** 87% positive of 6,547 all-time reviews, 74% recently ([store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/469600/Legion_TD_2/)).
  - Praise: "Chill, like a TD, yet deeply strategic"; "strategic, without all the micro"; "if you played it back in the day - you already bought this game"; ongoing development ([positive reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/positivereviews/?p=1&browsefilter=toprated)).
  - Veterans are split. One says "Worse than original wc3 map"; another says the game is "held back by how much it has preserved from Warcraft III" (LTD2 negative reviews).
- **Element TD 2:** 90% positive of 1,657 reviews; up to 8 in co-op; 59 towers ([store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1018830/Element_TD_2__Multiplayer_Tower_Defense/)).
  - Praise: "Brings back the good old WC3 mod days"; "Once you have two or more elements, things get interesting" ([positive reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1018830/positivereviews/?p=1&browsefilter=toprated)).
  - Criticism: "I find it boring even though I played Element TD a lot in WC3", the 6-element build, too few players, lag (ETD2 negative reviews).
  - Mazing is a paid expansion ([store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2177070/Element_TD_2__Mazing_Expansion/)), although mazing is "the one most former Warcraft 3 players actually miss" ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/games-like-legion-td-2)).
- **Others:** Tower Wars (2012) never kept the Line Tower Wars crowd ([Maul Tactics](https://maultactics.gg/articles/wc3-custom-maps-standalone-games)). YouTD 2 is in beta ([youtd.best](https://youtd.best/)).

### 5. Groundhold's choices through a veteran's eyes ([Inference], grounded as cited)

- **A fixed path with no mazing:** acceptable, since Legion TD proved it. Some veterans will still miss mazing. They will look for depth elsewhere: cells at corners and bends that are worth more, overlapping fields of fire, and splash where creeps bunch up. They will want ranges drawn on the board.
- **Builder kits:** familiar from Burbenog's governors, Wintermaul's races, Legion's races and YouTD's builders.
  - They will expect all-random, draft and no-duplicate rules (Legion's -fr is the same idea as Groundhold's unclashing).
  - They will want every kit roughly as strong; one Element TD wish was that "any combination of elements are playable with approximately equal power level" ([wish thread](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/294-if-you-only-had-one-wish/)).
  - They will hunt for the strongest Builder and call out any fork that is strictly worse.
- **Shared lives in co-op:** familiar from Wintermaul, and it brings blame with it. They will want leaks per seat, and Help or catches credited on the results screen, as Legion counted Leaks Caught.
- **Undertow's hidden sends:**
  - Precedents: Legion's Mastermind mode; an LTD2 guide calls hiding information "quite an effective weapon" ([guide](https://gameplay.tips/guides/legion-td-2-advanced-game-guide.html)).
  - They will test whether income from sends compounds into a decision by wave 5.
  - They will want a pack's armour readable once it is released.
  - They will judge whether the Seawall is a fair catch-up, like Line Tower Wars' catch-up gold, or plain rubber-banding.
- **The caretaker taking a left player's seat:** better than a lane going dark, but LTD2's bots are disliked. They "leak 100%", buy only workers, and "will never save up for sends". A moderator explained they "follow the recommended value" and "ignore attack and defense types" ([AI partner thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/5406000785145618389/), [bots/leaver thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/469600/discussions/0/1489992080520914881/)). One player found the bot "will have build everything for you perfectly" when he returned, which makes leaving a strategy.
  - They will expect a caretaker that answers armour types, banks for sends in Undertow, hands the seat back on rejoin, and still applies a leaver penalty.
  - LTD2 pauses for up to 60 s on a disconnect ([manual](https://beta.legiontd2.com/manual/)).

### 6. What delights them, and how they phrase feedback

**Delights:** element-style combinations; random and draft picks; difficulty switches; bonus creeps; a clear damage table; end scoreboards; a "-next" type preview; close games that reach late waves; a tutorial or sandbox; observer slots and ladders (wish thread, [3392](https://forums.eletd.com/topic/3392-why-element-td-is-awesome/)).

**Voice** ([Inference]): short, lobby-chat register, numbers first, comparisons to named maps. For example:
- "leaked 4 on wave 7, Winged came with no tell, need a -next"
- "fork B is strictly worse, trap pick"
- "income sends decide it by wave 5, worse than WMW"
- "caretaker held my lane but never banked a pack"
- "tooltip doesn't say Pierce or Siege, what's the armour?"
- "gg, close one"
