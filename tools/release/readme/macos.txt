Groundhold $version for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel)
=======================================================

A co-op tower defense for one to four players. MIT License, by Sascha Becker.
Source, changes and help: https://github.com/saschb2b/Groundhold


Install
-------

Drag Groundhold onto Applications, beside it in this window, then open it from your
Applications folder. (It runs on macOS 11 or newer on Apple silicon, 10.13 or newer on
Intel.)

The app is not notarised by Apple, so the first time macOS refuses to open it: "Apple could
not verify..." or "cannot be opened". Take the mark macOS puts on every app downloaded from
the internet (the quarantine) off in Terminal, once, then open the app as any other:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Groundhold.app

(with the app elsewhere, write its path instead: drag it into the Terminal window).

If an Apple silicon Mac says it "is damaged", sign it for this Mac alone and open it again:

       codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/Groundhold.app

Without Terminal: on macOS 15 and newer, after the first refusal, open System Settings,
Privacy & Security, and press Open Anyway beside Groundhold, then Open. On macOS 14 and
older, right-click (or Control-click) Groundhold in Applications, choose Open, then Open in
the dialog.

macOS asks once whether Groundhold may find devices on your local network and accept
incoming connections; Allow is only needed to play on your home network. Your name,
Builders and settings live in ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/Groundhold/.


Play together
-------------

Online: open Join (J) and press Go on the Official row
(wss://groundhold.saschb2b.com/causeway), or type a friend's party code. Friends in the
browser play at https://saschb2b.github.io/Groundhold/play/ and join the same parties.
The official server's rules, and what it keeps and for how long:
https://saschb2b.github.io/Groundhold/policy.html

On your network: one computer runs the server, groundhold-server, from the same release
page: groundhold-server-macos-arm64.zip on Apple silicon, groundhold-server-macos-x64.zip
on Intel (it needs the same xattr step, in its folder: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine
groundhold-server), or groundhold-server-python.zip with Python 3.11 or newer. Its window
prints the address to type; the game lists it under Join, Nearby. The others join with the
code on your strip. A firewall must let TCP port 8766 in, and UDP port 8767 for Nearby.
