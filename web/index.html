Lantern Hollow

A very small tower defense at dusk, in the spirit of Warcraft III's custom maps. Hold the winding road from the rift gate to the hearth with three towers, through eight waves and the Hollow King.

Play in your browser Source on GitHub

How to play

A C FChoose Arrow Tower, Cannon Tower or Frost Spire, then left-click a grass tile. The ghost turns red where you cannot build. Hold Shift to keep placing.
Click a towerSelect it: its range shows. U upgrades, S sells for 70% of what you paid.
Click a creepSee its health, speed and bounty in the console.
NCall the next wave early. The seconds left on the timer are paid as gold.
ViewArrow keys, the mouse wheel, middle-drag, or click the minimap. Home shows the whole hollow.
Right click, EscCancel building or the selection.

How it is made

Godot 4.7. Every model, icon, console frame and sound was generated for this game from recipes kept in the repository: low-poly models with painted bricks, shingles and planks, framed command icons rendered from those models, nine-slice frames, and synthesized sound effects. Nothing is taken from Warcraft III or any other game.

Gameplay is checked by deterministic scenarios: a scripted player must win all eight waves, an idle one must lose, and every rule (gold, leaks, waves, placement) has its own test. Fonts: Noto Serif, under the SIL Open Font License.
